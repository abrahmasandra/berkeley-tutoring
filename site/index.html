A little about me

Arnav Brahmasandra
Berkeley, California

My own studies have taken me from Chemistry Olympiad to computer science and statistics at UChicago, and now to chemical engineering at Berkeley. I like working at the point where a scientific idea and its mathematics start to make sense together.

I currently tutor students in calculus, high-school physics, and chemistry enrichment, with sessions shaped around what each student is learning or wants to explore. I've also mentored high-school students through UChicago's Data4All program. As co-president of UChicago Science Olympiad, I helped organize an invitational for 70+ middle- and high-school teams and wrote and graded Chemistry Lab exams.

In a session, we’ll start with your thinking.

Bring a problem, an attempted solution, or a topic that isn’t clicking. I’ll ask you to talk through your approach, work through the unclear steps with you, and have you try applying the idea to another problem. There’s room to slow down and ask why.
