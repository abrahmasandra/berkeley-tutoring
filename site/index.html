About me & how I tutor

Working through
the reasoning.

I enjoy rigorous problem-solving: figuring out why an argument works and what changes when its assumptions change. In tutoring, I want to help you understand ideas well enough to use them, rather than memorize a trick for one kind of question.

Bring a problem or topic you’re stuck on. I’ll ask you to talk through your thinking, work through the unclear steps with you, and have you try applying the idea to another problem.
